# @smartplant/spectrum

## 3.0.1

### Major Changes

- First release. Turns an RGB / multi-channel LED into a diagnostic instrument
  rather than illumination: each wavelength interrogates a different
  photoreceptor pathway, and the electrical response to each is diagnostic.

- 🔵 Blue reads stomatal competence (hydration), 🔴 red reads Photosystem II
  (photosynthetic capacity), 🟢 green penetrates to the deep mesophyll. Blue weak
  with red strong means thirst; red weak with blue strong means malnutrition — a
  distinction a single passive electrode cannot make.

- Therapeutic use is gated by hard physiological interlocks. Blue is refused on a
  plant with dry soil, because the plant closed its stomata to conserve water and
  forcing them open accelerates dehydration.

### Patch Changes

- Updated dependencies:
  - smartplant@3.0.1
